PRIVACY POLICY

Boo Notes

Effective 25 October 2026

Boo Notes ("Boo") is a notes app and text editor for Windows and Android, made by E (e@gmail.com). This policy explains what Boo does with your information. In short: your notes stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to us.

What Boo stores

  • Your notes and settings are stored on your device: on Windows in the Data folder next to Boo.exe, on Android in Boo's own app storage.
  • Boo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
  • Files you open from your computer stay where they are; Boo reads and writes them only when you open or save them.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Sign In with Google in Boo's Settings, Boo uses Google's sign-in to get permission to keep your notes in your Google Drive, so they appear on your other devices.

  • Permission requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets Boo see and change only the files and folders that Boo itself creates (a folder named Boo Notes and the notes and pictures in it). Boo cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • How the data is used: only to upload your notes and their pictures to your Drive, download them to your other devices, and move notes you close to your Drive's Trash. Boo also reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which account is signed in.
  • Where it goes: directly between your device and Google. Your notes and Google data never pass through, and are never stored on, any server operated by us. We cannot see them.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device, in Boo's data, so Boo can keep syncing without asking you to sign in each time.
  • Stopping: choose Settings › Sync › Sign Out in Boo to stop syncing on a device; your notes stay on the device and in your Drive. You can remove Boo's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the Boo Notes folder from your Drive yourself.

Google API Services

Boo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide Boo's sync to you, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person.

Children

Boo is a general-purpose notes app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: e@gmail.com.
