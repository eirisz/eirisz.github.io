Boo Notes

A notes app and text editor that never loses a note. Close it, crash it, switch devices: every tab is still there, saved or not.

What it does → Privacy policy

Boo walks along the bottom of the window. He's shy.

FEATURES

Built like Sublime, looks like iOS.

Nothing is lost

Every tab, including notes you never saved, comes back after you quit or the app crashes.

Rich text

Fonts, colours, lists, tables pasted from Excel, symbols and screenshots pasted straight into the note.

Find anything

Find in a note, in a folder or across every drive, with tags, Goto Anything and syntax colouring for 140 languages.

Huge files

Opens a 150 MB log in about two seconds. The Windows app is a 6 MB download that runs from a USB stick.

SYNC

Your notes, your Drive.

Sign in with Google in Boo's Settings and your notes are kept in a Boo Notes folder in your own Google Drive, so Boo on your laptop, tablet and phone shows the same notes.

Boo asks Google only for access to the files it creates itself. It can't see anything else in your Drive, and your notes never pass through any server of ours. See the privacy policy.
